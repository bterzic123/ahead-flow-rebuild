# Growth Audit — Ahead

**Live:** https://bterzic123.github.io/ahead-flow-rebuild/

Five ideas to unlock more revenue from the traffic Ahead already has, each one built into a clickable prototype of Ahead's own onboarding and paywall. The design is Ahead's: the ghost, the bar slider, the radar profile, the journey cards and the purple trial screen. Only the order changed.

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Put their goal on the price screen | CR +15–20% |
| 02 | Let them start before they sign up | CR +8% · ARPU +17% |
| 03 | Ask for reminders, Health and the widget after purchase | CR +10–15% |
| 04 | Put your award and your 70% next to the price | CR +10–15% |
| 05 | Offer Lifetime when they close | ARPU +10–15% |

Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps, not measured lift for Ahead. Every price, award, rating, quote and journey figure comes from Ahead's own App Store listing, website and in-app screens.
